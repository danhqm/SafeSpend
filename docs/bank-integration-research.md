# Malaysian bank-data connectivity research

Checked 18 October 2026. Advice and public-document research only; no bank connection, vendor signup, production test or legal determination was performed.

## Findings from primary sources

1. **BNM OpenAPI is public economic data, not a consumer bank feed.** BNM describes it as access to datasets already published on its website. Its free availability does not establish access to a person's bank transactions. [BNM OpenAPI FAQ](https://apikijangportal.bnm.gov.my/faq).

2. **Open Finance Malaysia is a real account-data initiative, but general production availability is unverified.** PayNet explicitly identifies personal finance management as a use case. BNM's current banking-document register still labels the November 2025 Open Finance document an exposure draft, alongside September 2026 updates. On 5 August 2026, BNM's Deputy Governor described open finance as entering its pilot phase the following year. Do not present a proposed rollout date as an available retail API. [PayNet OFM](https://paynet.my/digital-initiatives/open-finance-malaysia.html), [BNM document register](https://www.bnm.gov.my/banking-islamic-banking), [BNM August 2026 speech](https://www.bnm.gov.my/-/dgaaa-spch-rhb26).

3. **Published PayNet specifications cover accounts, balances and transactions.** The developer guide lists `/v1/accounts`, `/v1/accounts/{account_id}/balances` and `/v1/accounts/{account_id}/transactions`. It requires consented account IDs, access tokens, a PayNet-issued Data Consumer ID, transport certificates, request signing, response validation and decryption. These are specifications, not proof that SafeSpend can obtain production access. The guide was available in search-indexed content; direct fetching returned 502 during this review. [PayNet account-data guide](https://developer.sandbox1.prod.inet.paynet.my/en-gb/docs/developers-guide/09-accessing-account-data.md).

4. **Sandbox access and production access are different.** PayNet's developer-hub environment guidance says sandbox responses are simulated and do not connect to real participating institutions. UAT requires onboarding verification and formal conformance/certification, with successful certification making a participant eligible for production onboarding. PayNet's FAQ describes server-to-server retrieval through its API hub and end-to-end encrypted payloads. The official PayNet OFM page links to this Confluence knowledge centre; the detailed pages were readable in search-indexed excerpts but did not fully render through direct fetching. [Environment guidance](https://pay-net.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OFMDS/pages/1850146842/OFM%2BSandbox%2Band%2BUAT%2BEnvironments), [Participant FAQ](https://pay-net.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OFKB/pages/1551532051).

5. **The exposure draft is narrower than an unrestricted right for any app to read any bank.** Its definition of a data consumer is an FSP, and its listed FSP categories are regulated financial institutions. It proposes an initial effective date of 1 January 2027 with phased obligations, and prescribed data including the latest 12 months of transactions and current balances. Proposed consent controls include specific purposes, explicit opt-in, revocation and lifecycle management. These remain draft provisions in the source reviewed. SafeSpend's own eligibility or partnership route needs separate confirmation; the draft is not a licensing determination. [BNM exposure draft, definitions, sections 4 and 10, appendices 1–2](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/938039/ED_Open_Finance_2025.pdf).

6. **Payment APIs do not establish transaction-history access.** PayNet describes DuitNow Online Banking/Wallet as merchant payment acceptance, and DuitNow Consent as approval for automatic payment collections. Those documented purposes are different from reading a customer's full retail bank ledger. It is an inference from the API scopes, not a claim that PayNet has no account-data initiative. [PayNet payment API catalogue](https://docs.developer.paynet.my/api-reference), [Online Banking/Wallet overview](https://docs.paynet.my/docs/online-banking-wallet/overview).

7. **Importing bank data carries privacy work beyond a connect button.** JPDP identifies the PDPA's purpose as regulating personal-data processing in commercial transactions. Its DPO guidance includes thresholds and regular/systematic monitoring criteria, so a small user count alone does not settle applicability. Its cross-border guidance requires an applicable transfer condition and notice to individuals when data is transferred overseas. Applicability to SafeSpend, hosting locations, processors, retention, security, breach response and user-rights handling need review before real data flows. [JPDP Act overview](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/pdp-act-2010-en/), [DPO guidance](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GP_DPO_ENG.pdf), [Cross-border guidance, sections 4 and 14–16](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GP_CBPDT_EN-1.pdf).

## Implications and unresolved requirements

**Product inference:** bank import could reduce manual entry and improve completeness, but the sources do not justify making nationwide automatic sync an immediate promise. Validate demand and target-bank coverage before investing in production integration. A mock-data experiment can validate the consent and reconciliation experience without implying live availability.

**Vendor candidate identified separately:** Finverse's official Data API page advertises Malaysian individual-account support for CIMB, Maybank and Public Bank, together with balance, transaction and statement capabilities. This is the provider's published claim, not a verified bank-approved production connection. Its connection method, bank authorization, current account coverage, contracts, refresh reliability and total costs remain unverified. Do not repeat its inconsistent aggregate bank counts. [Finverse Data API](https://www.finverse.com/bank-data-api).

Before committing, establish the actual production admission/partner route, supported banks and account types, refresh frequency, history limits, stable transaction identifiers, reconnect/revocation behavior, pricing and operational support. Verify any provider's access method and permitted bank relationships directly rather than treating a coverage list as bank authorization.

For SafeSpend specifically, imported data must reconcile with existing opening balances, posted spending and transfers. A receipt/manual entry later found in the bank feed should remain one expense; transfers, refunds, pending-to-posted changes and imported current balances need explicit handling. Keep optional AI processing of imported bank data separately disclosed and consented. These are product/implementation recommendations inferred from the existing ledger and AI behavior, not something the public banking sources solve.

No vendor signup or live connection was attempted. No conclusion is made that a particular licence is required or unnecessary. Current production eligibility, bank agreements and legal applicability remain unverified.
